Ripe Leads Cold Email Writer

**Category:** Business & Operations

**Short description** (max 30 chars, this is 27):
Write cold emails, fix spam

**Description** (max 4000 chars; must stand alone as a tool description):

Writes and checks B2B cold emails. Five skills:

Cold email writer. Writes one cold email under 90 words with a subject line: opens on the prospect, uses one fact about their company, ends on one question. A short sequence on request.

Cold email audit. Scores a pasted email out of 100 (length, spam words, opening line, personalisation, value, call to action, tone) and returns a fixed version.

ICP builder. Takes a website and returns up to three target segments, job titles, buying signals and bad-fit exclusions, each marked verified or inferred.

Email deliverability check. Looks up SPF, DKIM, DMARC and MX for a domain and gives the exact DNS records to add.

Reply handler. Takes a prospect's reply and drafts one short answer in their language.

Not for bulk sending, buying email lists or scraping personal data. It writes and checks; you send.

**Default prompts** (up to 3, max 128 chars each; the manifest carries the first 3):
1. Write a cold email for my B2B service
2. Review my cold email and tell me why nobody replies
3. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC for my domain
4. Build an ideal customer profile from my website
